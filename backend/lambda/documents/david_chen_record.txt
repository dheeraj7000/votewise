BIOGRAPHY & CREDENTIALS:
David Chen serves as Council President of the Seattle City Council. He was first elected in November 2019 representing District 4, re-elected in 2023, and selected Council President by peers in January 2024.
Education: Bachelor of Arts in Urban Studies & Architecture from UC Berkeley (2006); Master of Urban Planning (MUP) from the University of Washington (2010).
Career: Prior to elected office, served as senior urban planner with Mithun Architecture & Urbanism (2010-2019) and Seattle Planning Commissioner (2015-2019).

KEY LEGISLATION & ACTIONS:
1. Seattle Middle Housing Ordinance (CB 120340): Authored and enacted landmark zoning measure allowing up to four residential housing units per parcel within 0.5 miles of frequent transit stops, unlocking infill cottage and courtyard construction across low-density neighborhoods.
2. Safe School Crosswalks & Automated Speed Enforcement (Res 32098): Sponsored initiative installing raised intersections and automated school safety cameras around 18 high-risk elementary school corridors.
3. Municipal Budget Audit Reform: Instituted biannual programmatic audits on department capital expenditure overruns, identifying $42 million in re-allocable surplus for downtown public space activation.

OFFICIAL STATEMENTS BY TOPIC:
- Housing & Zoning: In committee deliberations on May 10, 2022: "Permitting flexible missing-middle housing is the single most durable tool a municipality possesses to enable teachers, nurses, and service workers to afford living in the communities they serve."
- Public Transit & Micro-mobility: Remarks before the Sound Transit Board on February 19, 2026: "Reliable east-west bus feeder lines are the indispensable link that allows our light rail spine to reach workers outside the immediate urban core."
- Small Business & Commercial Corridors: Land Use Committee session on September 8, 2025: "Encouraging ground-floor small retail occupancy in mixed-use housing developments creates vibrant pedestrian corridors while expanding our municipal commercial tax base."
- Crisis Response & Public Safety: Public Safety oversight hearing on April 14, 2026: "We must accelerate non-sworn specialized crisis response teams for behavioral health dispatches so police officers can concentrate on urgent 911 emergency response times."
