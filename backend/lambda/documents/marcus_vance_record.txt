BIOGRAPHY & CREDENTIALS:
Marcus Vance has represented Washington State in the United States Senate since 2019. Prior to his election to federal office, he served in the Washington State Senate representing the 43rd Legislative District from 2013 through 2018.
Education: Vance earned a Bachelor of Science in Civil and Environmental Engineering from the University of Washington in 1998, and a Master in Public Policy (MPP) from the Harvard Kennedy School of Government in 2003.
Military Record: Served in the United States Navy Reserve (Civil Engineer Corps) from 2001 to 2009, attaining the rank of Lieutenant Commander before receiving an Honorable Discharge.

SPONSORED AND CO-SPONSORED LEGISLATION:
1. Microelectronics Regional Workforce Act (S. 2714): Authored and sponsored by Senator Vance. Enacted into law as Public Law 118-42. Authorized $1.2 billion in federal grants over four fiscal years to establish advanced semiconductor tooling and microelectronics technician laboratories across 14 state community college networks.
2. Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (P.L. 117-58): Co-sponsored. Negotiated specific western grid resiliency provisions, allocating $4.2 billion for seismic retrofitting of interstate highway bridges and electrical substation floodproofing.
3. Veterans Rural Tele-Mental Health Access Act (S. 3891): Co-sponsored bipartisan bill directing the Veterans Health Administration to deploy dedicated high-throughput satellite broadband stations to designated outpatient clinics across rural counties. Passed the Senate with 92 votes.
4. Clean Freight and Ports Act (WA State Senate SB 5831): Passed during state legislative service in 2015, modernizing deep-water port logistics and electrifying dockside heavy cargo handling machinery.

OFFICIAL STATEMENTS BY TOPIC:
- Affordable Housing & Infrastructure: In an address before the Washington State Association of Counties on May 4, 2025, Senator Vance stated: "Federal highway and transit allocations must prioritize local jurisdictions that reform restrictive exclusionary zoning around major rail and bus rapid transit hubs."
- Public Transportation: During the Senate Commerce Committee hearing on November 18, 2025 (Hearing 119-142), Vance testified: "Electrifying regional transit routes and expediting grade separation projects directly cuts commute times while reducing particulate emissions in frontline highway corridors."
- Education & Apprenticeships: Introducing S. 2714 on the Senate floor (172 Cong. Rec. S940), Vance declared: "Four-year degrees must not be the sole subsidized path to middle-class stability. Federal Pell grants must expand to accredited technical apprenticeships and high-voltage grid technician certifications."
- Healthcare & Rural Hospitals: In an official Senate press release on August 14, 2025, Vance stated: "Rural hospital emergency departments require targeted capital protection. When an obstetric unit closes in a county of forty thousand people, the resulting mortality rates are a systemic regulatory failure."
- Energy & Environment: Addressing the Western Power Grid Reliability Symposium on May 19, 2026, Vance noted: "Decarbonization must be paired with firm baseload capacity. We cannot replace coal and gas with intermittent sources without simultaneously deploying nuclear small modular reactors and pumped hydro storage."
- Public Safety & Fentanyl: In testimony before the Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on October 9, 2025, Vance asserted: "Fentanyl interdiction requires intercepting precursor shipments at container ports before distribution, paired with non-punitive regional crisis stabilization centers."
