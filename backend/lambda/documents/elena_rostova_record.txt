BIOGRAPHY & CREDENTIALS:
Elena Rostova serves as the 19th Attorney General of Washington, elected in 2020 and sworn in January 2021. Prior to state executive office, she served as Chief Deputy Prosecuting Attorney for King County from 2013 to 2020.
Education: Graduated Stanford University with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science and Economics (2001); earned a Juris Doctor from Columbia Law School in 2005.
Military Service: None.

EXECUTIVE ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS & REQUEST LEGISLATION:
1. Multistate Prescription Price-Fixing Settlement (March 2022): Spearheaded 16-state coalition resolving antitrust price-fixing claims against major pharmaceutical distributors, yielding $518 million dedicated to municipal opioid treatment facilities and naloxone distribution (King County Superior Court Consent Decree 22-2-04910-1).
2. Deceptive Student Loan Servicing Restitution (November 2023): Enforced $34 million restitution and balance discharges for 18,000 Washington borrowers misled by private loan servicers (Case No. 23-2-28491).
3. Request Legislation HB 1892 (Child Online Privacy Protection): Drafted and requested legislation prohibiting commercial digital platform behavioral profiling and algorithmic monetization targeting children under 16. Enacted as RCW 19.390.
4. Antitrust Injunction on Grocery Mergers (September 2024): Filed formal state antitrust suit to block the Kroger-Albertsons merger under the Washington Consumer Protection Act, citing price hikes in food deserts (King County Case No. 24-2-19280-4 SEA).

OFFICIAL STATEMENTS BY TOPIC:
- Healthcare & Prescription Costs: During an official consumer protection briefing on June 11, 2025: "State anti-price-gouging protections must apply strictly to prescription insulin, infant formula, and staple groceries during declared supply disruptions."
- Education & School Equity: Keynote remarks to the Washington State School Directors' Association on November 20, 2025: "Every student in Washington deserves equitable STEM facilities and special education funding regardless of zip code property tax assessments."
- Environment & Tribal Resilience: Address at the Pacific Northwest Climate Preparedness Summit on April 8, 2026: "Climate resilience investments must be targeted at coastal tribal jurisdictions and flood-prone agricultural valleys before severe seasonal atmospheric rivers occur."
- Public Safety & Retail Crime: Joint press conference with the Washington Association of Sheriffs and Police Chiefs on January 29, 2026: "Public safety requires combining rigorous organized retail crime task force enforcement with fully funded substance detox and mental health diversion facilities."
