DOCUMENT: OFFICIAL BALLOT TITLE AND FISCAL EXPLANATION - PROPOSITION 102
SOURCE: King County Elections & Sound Transit
VERIFICATION TIER: Tier 1 - Government
JURISDICTION: King County / Regional Transit Service District
LAST UPDATED: 2026-09-01

BALLOT TITLE:
Proposition No. 102 concerns funding for regional rapid transit feeder lines, safe pedestrian overpasses, and youth transit equity. This proposition would renew and replace the expiring 0.15% sales and use tax and assess a $25 annual vehicle excise license fee beginning in 2027 for eight consecutive years.

PROJECT COMMITMENTS:
1. Construction and operating capital for 4 new high-capacity East-West Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) feeder corridors connecting suburban residential areas directly to regional light rail stations.
2. Direct construction of 12 grade-separated, ADA-compliant pedestrian and bicycle highway crossings near designated high-collision transit centers.
3. Full funding for universal fare-free transit cards for enrolled students under age 21 across King County public school districts.

FISCAL DETAILS:
- Annual Estimated Yield: $82,000,000 per year over eight years.
- Household Cost Estimate: King County budget office calculates the combined tax and vehicle fee impact averages $34.00 annually per registered median household.
- Oversight: Requires independent annual citizen oversight committee performance audits and public dashboard reporting.
